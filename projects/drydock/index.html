Case study

The potable problem.

The challenge: “We have to reroute the water main on site and shut off service to our trailer compound for probably 45 days. We cannot tear up our compound decking and skirting. Can AMERI-CANS figure out how to get water to our trailers?”

We devised a 6,000-gallon potable water delivery system backflowed through a fire hydrant, pressurizing 200 yards of 8-inch main and some 600 yards of 1-inch and ¾-inch line, with NSF fixtures, pumps, a pressure tank and filters sourced and assembled over a weekend. Ideation to active in under four weeks. Decking and skirting untouched.
