About AMERI-CANS

Veteran DNA, applied to the humble porta potty.

The founders of AMERI-CANS are linked through that smallest of worlds, the United States Marine Corps, and an unexplainable desire to re-establish the benchmark for the lowly portable toilet.

Our story

Two infantry officers and a benchmark worth resetting.

Founders Patrick Ford and Will McLoughlin served simultaneously as USMC infantry officers and became fast friends through shared experience. Both had stints in finance and technology careers prior to serving in the military, and their bond was cemented during the best “management” training in the world. Austere environments provided ample opportunity to recognize the vastly under-appreciated value of portable sanitation.

In 2020, as founding partners of G.I. JOHNS, Patrick and Will recognized a need to expand their operations and incepted G.I. JOHNS’ Mid Atlantic to serve the DMV and surrounding areas. In March 2024, the Mid Atlantic division became the base of AMERI-CANS, and the rest is history in the making.

Our mission

Impact Veteran Outcomes. Support Gold Star Families.

AMERI-CANS’ mission is two-fold, Impact Veteran Outcomes (IVO) and support Gold Star Families, enabled by providing superior temporary sanitation solutions, grounded in our values of discipline and excellence. We excel through our commitment to execution, care, communication and problem-solving, without compromise. Our dedication to peerlessness drives us, and our success enables us to support fellow Veterans and Gold Star families, honoring our shared service and sacrifice.

Veterans

Ameri-Cans is focused on employing Veterans and their pursuit of post-service ambitions as productive members of society. We know the power of the harnessed purpose, initiative, tenacity and problem solving of this cohort. We also know what happens in the absence of the next challenge, task or obstacle; at best, they sit around throwing rocks at stuff (ask any Veteran) and worst case, they turn to nefarious outlets or succumb to hopelessness. We want to help, whether it is impacting 1 life, or 100. We will work to build a community within our community.

To us that means direct hiring with real, long-term opportunities for growth, success and stability.

Gold Star Families

AMERI-CANS was founded with a determined goal to support our nation’s Gold Star Families. A portion of the proceeds from each toilet goes to the Travis Manion Foundation and the Marine Corps Law Enforcement Foundation, and the GoldStar unit donates 100% of the proceeds from its use.

The Travis Manion Foundation runs multi-day Service Expeditions for Gold Star family members, built on a positive-psychology curriculum and community service projects. The Marine Corps Law Enforcement Foundation provides education for the children of service members who died in the line of duty.
