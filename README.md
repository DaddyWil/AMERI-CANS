# williamhans.app

Site of William "Billy" Hans, AMERI-CANS.

Flat files, no build step. Keep them side by side at the repository root, with the fonts/ folder alongside.

- index.html: personal landing, a terminal with the name in ASCII art and a menu (https://williamhans.app/). Built from Personal Landing.dc.html.
- ameri-cans.html: AMERI-CANS staff dashboard, formerly index.html (https://williamhans.app/ameri-cans.html). Stored encrypted like the tools: the file is a password screen with the dashboard inside, so nothing about the tools or site plans is readable without the team password. Behind it, collapsible groups, all closed by default and packed side by side: Products (every product page plus the two 3D models), Cut sheets (powered equipment PDFs and the plastics sheets), Forms, Site survey, Calculators, Pricing, Reports, Maps, Site plans, Training, and Branding. Each group opens to icon tiles. #products, #cut-sheets, #plastics, #calculators, #site-survey, and #maps open that group; #branding lands on Brand assets. Lock in the header locks the device again.
- products.html: the customer landing and the QR code target (https://williamhans.app/products.html), built from Products.dc.html. An open page with no password and no link to the private side: a showcase in the product-page look with every powered unit as a card with a photo and headline numbers, the twenty plastics as cards (name, group, best use; the sheets themselves are staff only), the cut sheet downloads, and Billy's contact card with a call button. The product pages link back here (All products, logo) rather than to the dashboard
- mark-iv.html: MARK IV product page (https://williamhans.app/mark-iv.html)
- cut-sheet.html: MARK IV Site Guide, the web version of the cut sheet (https://williamhans.app/cut-sheet.html)
- viewer.html: the 3D model embedded inside mark-iv.html, cut-sheet.html, the two site plans and the landing thumbnail (no longer linked on its own). It never traps the page: a plain scroll wheel over it scrolls the page past the model and one finger swipes scroll too; Ctrl (Cmd) + wheel, a trackpad pinch or two fingers zoom the model, a drag orbits it. URL flags: ?view=front|rear|interior|mechanical, doors=open, rotate=0, night=1, zoom=0 (no wheel zoom at all), sway=1 (a gentle sway instead of the turntable), dist=1.3. pump-house-viewer.html takes the same wheel rules
- model.html: Rough 3D model page with door controls, night mode, and OBJ/GLB download
- 8-stall.html, 10-station.html, 4-stall.html, ada-2.html, 8-stall-shower.html, water-station.html, bootwash.html: product pages for the powered equipment line (from the December 2025 cut sheets); pump-house.html: 500 Gal Pump House (from the Texas Operations water system diagram, September 2026, not in the cut sheet PDF; its hero is the 3D viewer); pump-house-viewer.html and pump-house-model.html: the pump house rough 3D model (embedded viewer, and the standalone page with door, night, trailer scene, and OBJ/GLB download); og-<name>.jpg is each page's link preview image
- fm600-hanson-rd.html: FM 600 & Hanson Rd sanitation plan for the RNG project, a twelve-stop walkthrough with maps, phasing, sizing, deployment schedule, rate card and cost profile, hookup schematic and cut sheets (https://williamhans.app/fm600-hanson-rd.html). Listed under Site plans on the dashboard, marked noindex, and the plan itself is stored encrypted: it opens only with an invited email address and its password (separate from the team password). The file is generated from the fm600-plan/ folder of the project (source, originals, sign-in list, build script), which is not part of the site. Its maps, photos and insets travel inside the encrypted file (about 7 MB); only the cut sheet images, fm600-cs_*.png, are plain files at the root. aws-atl-campus.html is the same kind of page for the AWS ATL campus, from the atl-plan/ folder, with its base map and the Georgia rate sheet PDF inside the encrypted file too
- powered-equipment-cut-sheets.pdf: AMERI-CANS Powered Equipment Cut Sheets, linked from every product page
- mark-iv-cut-sheet.pdf: original AMERI-CANS MK IV cut sheet
- logo-stacked.png, logo-horizontal.png, logo-fence.png, brand-color-palette.pdf: brand downloads (linked from behind the password)
- work-order-intake.html, water-waste.html, install-estimator.html, materials-pricing.html, site-survey-universal.html, site-survey-multiplex.html, maps.html, interview-form.html, fsl-interview.html, review-maintenance-manager.html (OPS-F05 yearly review), pricing.html, incident-report.html, training-certificate.html, folder-tour.html, water-usage.html (the RSE water log analysis): internal staff tools, stored encrypted; each shows only a password screen until unlocked
- plastic-<unit>.html (20 sheets) and plastics.html (the full set): the plastics fleet identification sheets from the Rental Gear Smartpack, stored encrypted like the tools (team password)
- fonts/: Archivo (body) and Archivo Wide (headings), self-hosted woff2, declared by wash.js so every page shares them; Arial is the fallback while they load or offline
- wash.js: shared background wash (faded service flags and brand marks) and the small animation set, loaded by every page except the 3D viewers and the game
- gate.js: password check used by ameri-cans.html (every gated page carries its own copy). It holds salts, encrypted check values and wrapped keys, never a password.
- support.js and ds-bundle.js: page runtime and component library, loaded by index.html, ameri-cans.html, mark-iv.html, and cut-sheet.html
- og-image.png: link preview image for iMessage, Slack, LinkedIn, and X (referenced by absolute URL from the pages)

The pages load React and three.js from unpkg.com, so visitors need an internet connection. The calculators and the site survey worksheets work offline once opened.

Night and daylight mode. One setting shared by every page on the site: landing, product pages, cut sheet, 3D model, lock screens, and all seven tools. It follows the device's dark mode until someone picks, then the choice is stored on that device (toggle in the landing header or the Night / Daylight button on any page); open tabs switch together.

Two sides. The customer side is open to anyone and links only to itself: index.html, about.html, products.html (the QR target), the product pages, cut-sheet.html, the 3D models, the cut sheet PDFs and brand files, service-rush.html. The private side is one team password (gate/password.txt in the project, or an alternate from gate/alt-passwords.txt): the dashboard, every staff tool and every plastics sheet are stored encrypted with the team key (AES-256-GCM; the key comes from the team password through PBKDF2), and the two site plans carry a copy of their page key wrapped under the same team key, so staff open them without an invited email (leave the email empty and enter the team password, or unlock the dashboard first). Invited customers still sign in to a plan with their email and password. Without a password the files are unreadable, even with the source open. A correct password is remembered on that device ("Stay unlocked on this device"); Lock on the dashboard clears it. Changing the team password means rebuilding every gated page and gate.js, then both plans; no password is in this folder.
