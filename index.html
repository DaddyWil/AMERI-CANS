Ameri-Cans uses an all-American color palette of red, white, and blue. The logo, graphics, and headlines should all take advantage of this primary palette.

Any of the blues can be used for headlines, but navy or deep sea blue will be a safe bet in any situation. All blues at a 10-20% opacity will create color blocks that text can run over. Use red sparingly, and only when needing extra emphasis.

Body copy should generally use black and/or other darker shades from the neutral palette. Too much color can look childish. Pure black and Dark Gray will be best for digital body copy.
