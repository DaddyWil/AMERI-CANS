William "Billy" Hans

Philadelphia raised. US Navy Hospital Corpsman. Now running a multi-branch operation across Texas.

Where I came from, and what made me. ↓

01whans@williamhans:~/about$

Born in Philadelphia.

Raised by my father, William, and my mother, Genine, in a working-class family. Never rich, and never wanted for anything, because of my parents' sacrifices.

Grade school at St. Nicholas of Tolentine. That's where my morals and standards were formed.

02whans@williamhans:~/about$

Technology came easy.

I was lucky to grow up alongside it, from LeapFrog tablets to home-built servers. Very capable with a keyboard.

03whans@williamhans:~/about$

Always on a field.

Sports and extracurriculars, always. Football and baseball at the higher levels for kids my age.

04whans@williamhans:~/about$

Two years in a classroom. The rest in under one.

Preparatory Charter High in Philly for two years. Then online school, where I finished the remaining coursework in under a year and took the diploma.

05whans@williamhans:~/about$

Hands first.

Diploma in hand: a laborer for a contracting company alongside a family friend, then an A-driver and driver for Samuel and Sons Seafood, a seafood wholesaler.

06whans@williamhans:~/about$

US Navy. Hospital Corpsman.

Walter Reed National Military Medical Center. Enlisted lead for pediatrics, dual-hatted as the building's safety leader and the command fitness leader.

07whans@williamhans:~/about$

Then, the duty office.

Toward the end of my tenure I ran the command duty office: the duty rotations, and securing the facility through COVID.

I was also on an exclusive team that made sure officials from the White House could receive care without it becoming a public show.

08whans@williamhans:~/about$

Out, and on my own.

After the service I worked for myself as a fitness professional, earning certifications across specialized roles for different age groups and goals.

In between, a brief stint with a security company as account manager for Whole Foods locations across the Philadelphia area.

09whans@williamhans:~/about$

Then, the buildout.

Project management next: Assistant Project Manager on the construction of a casino just outside the city.

10whans@williamhans:~/about$

Regional Operations Manager.

I started at AMERI-CANS as an Assistant Operations Manager and worked my way into Regional Operations Manager for the South Central region: a multi-branch operation expanding across Texas.

11whans@williamhans:~/about$

How I run it.

The technical skill set comes from years of hobby tinkering. The rest is critical thinking, problem solving, and staying calm under pressure. Put together, that's how I manage a multi-branch operation that keeps growing.
