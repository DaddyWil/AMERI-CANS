    Optional adders Not in the workbook

    The waste model in the workbook totals raw cost only: supplier prices plus labor, with no sales tax and no margin. (The separate fresh water kit tabs add 6% tax and a 20% upcharge.) Leave these at 0 to match the workbook exactly.

    Materials and kit quantities

    Unit prices are the workbook's 10/16/2025 list. Quantities are what the workbook charges per stub-out or per tank. Pipe prices are shared wherever the same pipe is used.
