Laid out for your site. Filled from the outside.

Every pump house is built for the project it serves: tank, pumps, and line runs laid out for your trailer and your site. The camlock fill connection and overflow outlet sit on the front wall, so the tank fills without opening the door.

Inside, a 500 gal potable holding tank certified to NSF/ANSI/CAN-61 feeds two delivery pumps on a valved manifold. Rigid foam insulation lines the walls and roof.

Heat traced to your trailer.

Supply lines run from the pump house to your trailer wrapped in insulation with heat trace underneath, at the length your layout needs. The heat trace plugs in at the trailer.

Waste out the same way.

A 3 in Sch. 40 PVC drain line runs from the trailer to insulated waste holding tanks, extending capacity beyond the trailer's onboard tank.
